# Hello, I'm Your Name

I'm a passionate web designer and developer with over 5 years of experience creating beautiful, functional websites and applications.

## My Background

I started my journey in design and development when I was in college, studying Computer Science with a minor in Graphic Design. This unique combination gave me a strong foundation in both the technical and creative aspects of web development.

After graduating, I worked for a digital agency for three years, where I had the opportunity to work on projects for a variety of clients across different industries. In 2023, I decided to pursue freelance work, which has allowed me to work directly with clients and take a more hands-on approach to each project.

## My Approach

I believe that great design is about more than just aesthetics—it's about creating solutions that are:

- **User-centered:** Understanding the needs and goals of the end users
- **Purposeful:** Solving real problems through thoughtful design
- **Accessible:** Creating experiences that work for everyone
- **Sustainable:** Building with longevity and maintainability in mind

Every project I take on begins with research and planning to ensure I fully understand the goals and constraints before diving into design and development.
