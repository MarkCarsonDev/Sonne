# Welcome to Sonne

This is your first blog post using the Sonne static site generator. You can edit this post by modifying the `content/blog/2025-01-01-welcome-to-sonne.md` file.

## Getting Started

Sonne makes it easy to create blog posts. Simply add Markdown files to the `content/blog` directory with a filename in the format `YYYY-MM-DD-title.md` and front matter at the top.

### Front Matter

The front matter is the YAML section at the top of the file between the `---` markers. It contains metadata about your post:

```yaml
---
title: Welcome to Sonne
date: 2025-01-01
author: Your Name
tags:
  - sonne
  - getting started
categories:
  - announcements
---
```

### Writing Content

After the front matter, you can write your content in Markdown format. Sonne supports standard Markdown syntax:

- **Bold text** and *italic text*
- Lists (like this one)
- [Links](https://example.com)
- Images: ![Alt text](/path/to/image.jpg)
- And much more!

## Features

Sonne's blog template includes several features:

1. **Responsive Design**: Your blog will look great on any device
2. **Tags and Categories**: Organize your posts
3. **Pagination**: Navigate through multiple pages of posts
4. **RSS Feed**: Allow readers to subscribe to your content
